Strange Creatures · 01

The Hardest Place to Go

The hardest place to go? Tomorrow We have managed to name ourselves and where we are. The universe is 5% light. Everything has already happened, but we can't see it yet. Things are too far away and the speed of light is too fast. So we reduce everything to meaningless time frames. We construct things we can control. But they are powerless against understanding anything real. It doesn't matter. Colossal squid are everywhere.

Strange Creatures · 02

Mallard

A dullard or a mallard, both use their beaks to identify themselves. One will wake you up, the other put you to sleep. One has evolved a corkscrew phallus, like only a small percentage of birds. It navigates the convoluted, clockwise-spiralled vaginas of female ducks. Yet I will not follow many conversations.

Strange Creatures · 03

Platypus

Slippery rocks do not forgive. Tie your shoelaces. Do platypuses, alive, believe they have a place to live, and leave no traces, and still have patience when barefoot people, with GoPros, lacking graces, stalk the forest in the places they hide! Near lakes! Eat flies and worms! Have venomous spikes! And want to be alone. On the ground lie mangos by the pavement. We have gone too far and should not be here.

Remains · 07

Teeth

Many years ago, a man from Iran said my teeth looked natural. I had braces at fourteen. The results were great. I had a lovely girl and a smile to match. I wore a plastic mouthpiece, but night for night they shifted back. I run my tongue across my teeth, trying to map the changes from year to year. I don't think about it. It happens. Today it feels like they don't fit. I don't know him anymore. He left for Australia, but came back. I never saw him again.

Remains · 08

Emergency

In an emergency— or rather, the documents for it— there are no adjectives. Just nouns, and maybe verbs. Only after everyone has sent a pamphlet of instructions to one another can we be sure that no one is to blame. Unless the insurance man asks. Then it wasn't me, but definitely someone. When we are finished with the reports, we can start on their file.

Remains · 10

Racist

It is jarring meeting racists in bars. You may want to glass them, or ask them what they are. What left them scars? What gave them blackened hearts? When will they be happy? Would a happy racist know itself? I live vicariously in YouTube videos, watching people camp or cycle through places I increasingly feel I will never see. Should I learn to hate too? Or am I dulled? Am I scarred with a blackened heart? If you see me cursing others late at eighty in a shoddy bar, wishing I was younger and others were more miserable, take pity please. I'm not really bad. I'm just very sad.

Remains · 14

Cycling

In the shadow of the Rip Mountain, from Dresden to Leitmeritz, the rivers merging in Melnik, to Shakespeare's in Prague, then a ham sandwich in Molly Malone's. I remember the Elbe opened up. There were lily pads at the edges of a deep valley. I felt free. I used Clif Bars to keep going, learned to love salted peanut oats and tinned sausages. I rode on. I stayed, cycling away from the stench of home, after changing jobs and feeling alone. Still, I went back, after the permitted two weeks of holiday.

Remains · 15

Windsor Drive

Windsor Drive turned 90 degrees, the A40 on the right, the Scirocco on the left, wheel chained to the seat, no longer full of CDs after we had moved. We had already been to the Giant's Causeway, that day. Rory guitaring with the Micro Cube. Lauren on her hind legs, back from Hunter's. So far from the end, the day closing in. I remember we cooked lentils, and it was rotten. I was never happier.

Remains · 19

Cow Tracks

Cows make tracks on the sides of hills when they walk from hedge to hedge, then lie down before the rain comes. When they change fields, the grass grows back. The indent on the hillside stays. The river at the bottom flows on and on. The valley widens. Then one day it will be a desert. You, me and the birds and bees, the tracks of the cows, the grass, wouldn't even be a memory, not a memory to be, not a thing to see.

Stripy Tops

When she wears stripy tops, it's like topographical lines around her breasts. I run my fingers round them, like exploring maps. A land I know, still explore, and never feel peace in. It's being raised a Protestant, you see. Is that what it means? Work hard and prosper. Live within your means. Don't forget to worry. Raise the road up to meet you. Were it easy, we'd not feel.

Sure You Have Your Health

Sure you have your health, people say, but I just want to die. When I see heart attack pictures on the packages of cigarettes, I smoke another one, wait for the tingle in my arm, and pray. "Give me strength," I hear. What a disappointment. He has let his family down. He's made the priest frown. We'll not lament that. No one asked him, was there another way?

Girls

The girls I have loved have all had depression. The tender suffering in caring for yourself, limitlessly unwilling to move, in dark obsession, wanting nothing, the desperate rebuilding of a human soul. We sit in silence, drift closer and farther until the days brighten. They disappear.

Home · 23

Belfast

If the windowsills in Belfast were neat and level in a row, would it still be home, somewhere I know? Looking down the uneven, uniform rows of terrace houses, we had the space to grow in the small alleyways, too loud for the call of the crow. When the bins were emptied on Thursdays, and the oil tank stayed empty. In the early morning dew, in the corner garden, after the barbecue.

Airport Bar

It is barely half a life, living half here and half there. And the half I'm in is never the half where I want to be. I can leave neither and be in neither, downing Guinness in the airport bar, wishing to be near, wishing to be far, always with the squeeze of time in your neck.

Home · 25

The Earth and Stars

If you cannot toss my body in the sea, throw me in a hole in Louth, in the old abandoned church, for tree roots to take me, and fungus, back to memories of darts and pints in Emo's, followed by a Chinese. Instead of travelling the tides, in the Atlantic's long breaths, from the coasts of home through the vast free oceans to other shores, I will float away, in slow decay, back to the time when there was only happiness. You were made for this game, Daniel. I will see you there, boys, in the earth and stars.
