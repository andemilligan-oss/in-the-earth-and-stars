Crows

Me da said crows follow him everywhere he goes, there's crows Often in a quiet moment I'll look around me I'll see one crow doing normal crow business seeming shifty, but actually just being a crow on a roof, or a railing or hopping along the ground I'll wonder what my dad is doing I'll worry something's happened I'll say, "alright dad" then we carry on.
