The Tax

I'm scared of the day we only meet for funerals, Until now the days were perfect even when they weren't — When you realise. how much can fit in one warm summer. When you see one swallow and it is enough; one warm breeze with the people you love The voice, or the smile your granda gave you with his eyes I'm scared of missing you, but can't hold moments tighter even when the warm blood through our veins makes life plain, and simple. It is only love if you let it be, then distance and time lead us here, to fear. I'm scared of a time we only meet for funerals.
